LaTeXOutline v. 2.6.2 -- Changelog

- Removes the alt+click behaviour that was undocumented and unreliable

- Adds instead a new boolean option in the settings: "copies_label_with_refcommand"
	false: when '❐' is pressed, copies label to the clipboard
	true: when '❐' is pressed, copies \ref{label} or \eqref{label} is copied to the cliboard

- Corrects a bug where a section title with several lines could shift the outline links to the LaTeX file
